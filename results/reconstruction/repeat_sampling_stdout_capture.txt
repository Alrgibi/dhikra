Q1  --  does averaging cut SEM by sqrt(k)?
============================================================================
  MDC95 = 1.96 * sqrt(2) * SEM   (two occasions compared)
  check: 1.96*sqrt(2)*0.1032 = 0.2861  vs published 0.286  -> arithmetic confirmed

    k    SEM_k    MDC95   reduction
    1   0.1032   0.2861        0.0%
    2   0.0730   0.2023       29.3%
    3   0.0596   0.1652       42.3%
    4   0.0516   0.1430       50.0%
    6   0.0421   0.1168       59.2%
   10   0.0326   0.0905       68.4%

  ANSWER: yes -- IF the k recordings are independent measurements of the same state.
  k=2 gives MDC 0.202, i.e. 0.286 -> 0.202. The stated arithmetic holds.
  CEILING CAVEAT: sqrt(k) assumes INDEPENDENT errors. Two recordings taken minutes
  apart share room, prompt delivery, fatigue and mood, so their errors are positively
  correlated and the real reduction is SMALLER than sqrt(k). With error correlation rho,
  the variance of the mean of 2 is SEM^2*(1+rho)/2, so:
    rho=0.0 -> SEM_2=0.0730, MDC=0.202
    rho=0.2 -> SEM_2=0.0799, MDC=0.222
    rho=0.4 -> SEM_2=0.0863, MDC=0.239
    rho=0.6 -> SEM_2=0.0923, MDC=0.256
  Pitt has NO within-session repeats, so rho CANNOT be estimated from this data.
  0.202 is therefore an UPPER BOUND on the benefit, not a measured value.

Q2  --  what MDC would individual TRACKING require?
============================================================================
  observed progression among the impaired: 0.0447 per visit = 0.0372 per year
  (individual variation is large: SD of per-visit change = 0.196)

    k     MDC  years to detect  min detectable rate/y  x group mean
    1   0.286              7.7                  0.286           7.7
    2   0.202              5.4                  0.202           5.4
    4   0.143              3.8                  0.143           3.8
   10   0.090              2.4                  0.090           2.4
   41   0.045              1.2                  0.045           1.2

  To detect ONE YEAR of average progression you need MDC <= 0.0372.
  Required k = (MDC_1/0.0372)^2 = 59 recordings per timepoint.
   over 1 year(s): need MDC <= 0.037  ->  k = 58.9
   over 2 year(s): need MDC <= 0.074  ->  k = 14.7
   over 3 year(s): need MDC <= 0.112  ->  k = 6.5
   over 5 year(s): need MDC <= 0.186  ->  k = 2.4

  ANSWER: averaging two recordings takes the minimum individually-detectable
  progression from 7.7 years to 5.4 years. Both are far outside clinical use.
  The 'screening test, not monitoring test' conclusion SURVIVES repeat-averaging.
