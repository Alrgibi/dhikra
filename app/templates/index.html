Speech-based screening for cognitive decline
Step 1

Participant details

Age and education are recorded because both influence speech measures and must be accounted for when interpreting results.

Step 2

Which surahs does the participant know by heart?

The recitation task probes overlearned memory — material rehearsed since childhood, which is preserved far longer in Alzheimer's than everyday speech. It only measures preservation if the person genuinely knows the passage, so select only what they have actually memorised. The app then chooses one of them.

Step 3

Before you begin

Consent. Explain that their voice will be recorded to measure how they speak, that recordings stay on this device, that taking part is voluntary, and that they may stop at any time. Do not proceed without their agreement.
This is a screening aid, not a diagnosis. ذِكرى cannot diagnose Alzheimer's disease or any other condition. It indicates whether a clinical assessment is worth seeking.

Find a quiet room. Hold the device about 30 cm away. Let silences stand — pauses are part of what is being measured.
